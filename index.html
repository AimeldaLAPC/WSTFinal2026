Our story

More than taho. It’s a taste of home.

For many Filipinos, taho is part of the soundtrack of childhood: the familiar call of a street vendor, warm silken tofu, sweet arnibal, and sago pearls shared on a quiet morning.

Pinoy Taho LA celebrates that tradition and the Filipino community in Los Angeles. Our goal is to make it easier to enjoy a comforting favorite, share a memory, and feel a little closer to home—even on the busiest day.
